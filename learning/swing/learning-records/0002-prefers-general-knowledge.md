# Prefers general Swing knowledge over project-specific framing

Status: active — supersedes the framing (not the content) of [[0001-no-prior-swing-experience]]

After Lesson 1 and the reference doc were built heavily wired to the hotel booking project (task numbers, specific file names like `BaseTableModel`/`swing-guide.md`), the user said: "no just a knowledge don't need to to much related to the project." They want durable, general Swing knowledge, not a project-specific cheat sheet.

## Implications
- [[MISSION.md]] updated: success criteria are now general Swing competence, not "can finish task X.Y."
- Future lessons and reference material should teach concepts on their own terms. Light, occasional project examples are fine if they clarify a point, but the project's task list should not be the spine of the curriculum.
- Existing project-tied material (`lessons/0001-...html`, `reference/swing-cheatsheet.html`) should be revised to drop task/file-number references where practical.
